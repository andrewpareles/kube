This Privacy Policy outlines how the website Kube.new ("the Website"), which is owned and operated by Glass Devtools, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, protects, and shares information about you when you access or use our Website. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with this policy. 

## 1. Collection of Personal Information

When you register or interact with our services, we collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, and any additional information you provide voluntarily. We do not directly collect passwords or other secure data - this is done through our trusted partners.

## 2. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer or mobile device by a website's server. We use cookies to authenticate users, remember preferences, and tailor our services to provide a better user experience. Cookies help us understand how you interact with our site, enabling us to improve our services based on user behavior.

## 3. Information We Collect

**Your account.** When you sign up, we store your email address and username. Your password is held by our login provider, and we never see it. We also store account information like the day you joined, how many editor AI credits you have left, your profile's description, and other fields you provide us. We use third parties to handle username/password and payment information.

**Games you make.** The games you build are stored on our servers, along with their thumbnails, and the number of times each one has been played and liked. We store the AI chats you use when building your game, and all file uploads from you and the AI.

**Playing games.** When you join a game, we record that play session, including how long you played. We may publicly display the games you play. We may store chat messages you send and other actions you take while playing the game.

**Cookies.** A cookie is a text file stored in your browser. We use cookies on our website to remember games you played and edited, handle launching of the desktop app, and track basic analytics, device information, and usage.

**Analytics.** We use analytics to understand how people use our website and desktop app so that we can improve. We can see all the actions you take on the website, how long you play each game, and messages you send to the AI. We may also receive information about your device and approximate location based on your IP address. When you are logged in, this information is linked to your user ID.

## 4. Information Games Collect

When you join your game or someone else's game, that game can access your username, user ID, and basic information necessary to run the game like location, camera angle, and inputs being pressed. Game servers may store derived information about your account like scores, leaderboards, and time played. This information is currently temporary and erased when the server shuts down.

## 5. Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted partners and service providers, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential. This includes analytics for traffic analysis. We may also share information when it's necessary to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect our or others' rights, property, or safety.

## 6. Account Deletion

If you want to delete your account - and the information associated with it - you may do so by contacting us at support@kube.new. Please contact us using the email address associated with your account, requesting the deletion and confirming your consent to lose all the information stored on your account.

## 7. Your Public Information

Other people can see your username, profile information, and games you publish. 
Your email is never shown to others. The games you've marked private are never shown to others.  Anyone can read your public profile and published games, and search engines can list them.

## 8. Children Under Thirteen

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of thirteen.

## 9. Updates and Changes

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@kube.new.

If we change this page, we will change its last-updated date.
