Think about the most fun you've ever had in a video game. You're probably not thinking of a mobile game, or a single player game, even though those can be fun. You're probably thinking of a game you played online with friends.

Fun comes from multiplayer!

We put together a tier list of items we think are good, bad, and neutral in a multiplayer game. Kube.new is early, but we keep these in mind after every update.






### Bad



1. Pay-to-win. It usually defeats the fun of playing when someone can just buy an item to win every round.

2. Play-to-win. Creators usually don't care if their players are having fun, if they simply want them to spend N hours playing before unlocking the next item.

3. Fake Humans. The fun of multiplayer comes from connecting with (or competing with) other humans. Bots are only OK if they are clearly labeled, and not there to deceive you into thinking you're playing with people.


### Neutral

1.  Types of novelty that are temporarily entertaining, but not truly fun. Amazing graphics, cutscenes, novel visual effects, sounds, humor. You obviously want these in a good game, but they do not create fun on their own, and can keep you playing a game simply out of novelty. 

The ultimate test of whether a game was fun is how it ages. Fall Guys and Mario Kart both have great graphics, but one was hype, and one is real fun. 



### Good
1. The #1 source of fun is player-to-player interaction. If you can jump on, bump into, or shoot your friends, the game is more fun. This is the most important feature of any truly fun game, and it's what we focus on in our engine.

2. Indirect player-to-player interaction. Leaderboards, trading, etc. You can get very fun dynamics from this.

3. High skill caps. Depth. Emergent behavior. These all make the game worth playing often.

4. Free will. It's annoying if you drop an item for your friend but they're not able to pick it up, or if you try to jokingly damage them but you're not allowed. If a player has free will, a game should limit them minimally.


## Kube.new

On Kube.new, you're free to build any game - "good" or "bad". But we promise to do our best supporting games with the good features above, without needing to build the bad ones.

We think the world is ready to bring back creative, awesome, novel, and truly fun multiplayer games.
