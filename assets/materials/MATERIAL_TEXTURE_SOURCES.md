# Material Texture Sources

This page lists free source pages for PBR texture sets we can later turn into game-maker materials. I did not download the texture archives yet.

## License-Safe Sources

- [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) - [CC0 1.0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Strong default source. Most selected assets include color, normal, roughness, displacement, and sometimes AO, metalness, emission, or opacity.
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) - [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Strong default source for high resolution scanned textures. Individual texture pages usually expose 4K/8K+ map downloads.
- [TextureCan](https://www.texturecan.com/) - [CC0 1.0](https://www.texturecan.com/terms/). Useful for specific PBR texture categories. Its terms allow redistribution with projects and 3D assets.
- [3DTextures.me](https://3dtextures.me/about/) - [CC0](https://3dtextures.me/about/). Useful for gap categories like water and glass. Many free downloads are 1024 px, with higher resolution sometimes behind Patreon, so treat these as fallback picks.
- [ShareTextures](https://www.sharetextures.com/p/license) - custom CC0-style terms with direct-download and redistribution restrictions. I am not using it as a primary source until we decide those terms are acceptable for shipped game assets.

## Gaps To Revisit

- Goo/slime is the biggest gap. I did not find a clean, high-resolution, CC0 PBR slime/goo set from the main safe libraries. The listed options are wet-ground and wet-decal proxies. For a game-maker material, a small procedural shader plus normal/noise textures will probably be cleaner than pretending wet mud is slime.
- Water as a true material is also usually better as a shader with animated normal maps, refraction, opacity, and color controls. The listed water textures are good enough for static/simple water, but not enough for polished ocean/river rendering by themselves.
- Glass should probably be split into two game materials later: transparent glass controlled by shader properties, and patterned/stained glass using color/opacity/normal textures.
